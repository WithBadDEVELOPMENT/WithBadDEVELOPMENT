How we got here

Every version changes something. Here is where the current build came from.

v0.1

Monkey Fan Group Bloxburg

Founded by 12aaron1221 as a casual hangout spot with no real projects yet.

v1.0

With Bad STUDIOS

The group became a proper studio and started on DOORS With Bad and Poppy Playtime With Bad.

v1.5

Scary Baboon With Bad

The project brought more attention to the team and became one of its biggest.

v2.0

The team expands

Developers, moderators, testers, composers, artists and contributors joined.

v3.0

Full release

Website release and a full rebrand.

v4.0

Full recode

The website was rebuilt from the ground up with a green terminal look, more animation, cleaner pages and a dedicated help section.

v5.0, current build 5.964.56

Full recode two

Smoother animations, a reworked loading screen, quicker redirects, hardware acceleration fixes, a full UI patch, a reworked help screen, a new applications page, and now a site assistant.
